### About

I taught myself to build software in 2022. Since then I have shipped online shops with M-Pesa payments, a point-of-sale system, business websites and a public-finance transparency platform.

I work the whole stack: database schema, API, interface and deployment. I start with how the business actually runs, then choose the tools. The result is mobile-first and payment-ready by default.

I also run [ABC Systems](https://abcsystems.co.ke/), a technology company in Nairobi that supplies websites, business software, automation and hardware to Kenyan businesses.
